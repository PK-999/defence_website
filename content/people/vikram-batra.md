Captain Vikram Batra, PVC (9 September 1974 – 7 July 1999) was an officer of the Indian Army. He was posthumously awarded the Param Vir Chakra, India's highest military decoration, for his actions during the 1999 Kargil War.

His famous radio message "Yeh Dil Maange More" (This heart wants more) became the catchphrase of the Kargil War.
