# Indian defence equipment research snapshot

Generated by `src/equipment_scratch.py` on 15 September 2026 (UTC).

The snapshot contains 1,413 in-scope records from 12 registered source pages and 114 raw tables covering Indian Army, Indian Navy, and Indian Air Force equipment, weapons, aircraft, ships, historical systems, active systems, and named future programmes. It also includes 15 curated official-source claims and 14 clearly labelled official supplement records from PIB/Ministry of Defence and DRDO releases.

## Files

- `equipment_records.csv` — canonical, source-attributed records.
- `equipment_records.json` — the same records with structured `raw_record` values.
- `raw/*.csv` — cleaned copy of every parsed source table.
- `official_verification.csv` / `official_verification.json` — primary-source claims used to audit current status, contracts, trials, roles, and commissioning updates.
- `source_manifest.json` — registered source URLs and their declared scope.
- `run_report.json` — retrieval time, table counts, status counts, and excluded rows.

## Field conventions

- `domain` is the normalized primary service domain: `army`, `navy`, or `airforce`. Joint records use the first source-listed service as the primary domain, retain every applicable service in `service_domains`, and receive a `joint-service` tag; `service_domains` is authoritative for filtering joint systems.
- `equipment_domain` preserves the source's broad equipment area, while `category` uses controlled values such as `aircraft`, `ships`, `submarines`, `missiles`, `artillery`, `armoured-vehicles`, `air-defence`, and `support-equipment`.
- `service_status` is the display vocabulary: `Deployed`, `Decommissioned`, or `Planned`. The original normalized lifecycle state remains in `status`, and the source wording remains in `source_status`.
- `tags` is a deterministic JSON list containing service, category, lifecycle, role, and verification tags.
- `quantity_raw` preserves the source wording; `quantity_min` and `quantity_max` are populated only when numeric bounds are explicitly present.
- `quantity_values` preserves every explicit numeric quantity when a source combines multiple variants or order states in one cell.
- `source_category` preserves the source heading/type used to derive the controlled `category`.
- `source_status` preserves the source wording used to derive the normalized `status` and display `service_status`.
- `verification_status` is `official_claim_available` when a curated official claim matches the system, `official_supplement` for a separately sourced official record, and `not_independently_verified` when no matching primary-source claim was added.
- `verification_sources` lists the official URLs matched to the row; it is not a claim that every field in the row is independently verified.
- `specifications`, `dimensions`, and `raw_record` retain source fields that do not fit the canonical columns.
- Dates use ISO text (`YYYY`, `YYYY-MM`, or `YYYY-MM-DD`) so a missing day or month is never invented.
- Missing values are `Not documented`; they are not estimated.

The active-aircraft source page also contains four Coast Guard rows. Those rows remain in its raw CSV for auditability but are excluded from the Army/Navy/Air Force canonical export. The supplemental Army legacy source is a secondary-source API snapshot; it adds retired/legacy records not present in the supplied current-inventory page, but it cannot prove a complete historical Army inventory. The official supplement rows are additive and do not silently rewrite table-sourced rows. This is a source-backed research snapshot, not a globally authoritative or permanently complete inventory; future procurement and service status should be refreshed before publication.
