# LCA Tejas Official Specifications

The HAL Tejas is an Indian single-engine, delta wing, light multirole fighter designed by the Aeronautical Development Agency (ADA) in collaboration with Aircraft Research and Design Centre (ARDC) of Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL).

## Performance Data
- **Maximum speed**: Mach 1.8 (1,980 km/h) at high altitude
- **Range**: 1,850 km (1,150 mi)
- **Combat range**: 500 km (310 mi) with internal fuel
- **Ferry range**: 3,200 km (1,980 mi) with drop tanks

## Service
It was officially inducted into the Indian Air Force in 2016, specifically with No. 45 Squadron IAF "Flying Daggers".
