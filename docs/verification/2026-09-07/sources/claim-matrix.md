## Explicitly withheld or disputed

- Casualty totals are omitted. `IE-KARGIL-2024` reports the commonly cited official figure of 527 Indian dead, while `PIB-KARGIL-2025` uses 545 martyrs and later breaks that number into personnel and civilians. This needs a scoped definition and a primary casualty roll before publication.
- An exact Operation Vijay start day is omitted because official commemorative accounts and secondary timelines use different milestones for discovery, mobilisation, and the start of offensive action.
- The claim that the FH-77B was used at Kargil is common and plausible, but the captured official FH-77B source proves procurement and variant context rather than its Kargil employment. A direct operational source is still needed.
- Wikipedia-only aircraft sortie counts, squadron commanders, living-person details, and equipment performance values are not imported.

## Automated publication rule selected from the user's instruction

Routine facts do not require individual manual approval. A candidate may advance automatically only when it has either one directly relevant Tier A official source, or two independent reputable sources, and no unresolved contradiction. Disputed claims, living-person current details, exact casualty counts, and media rights remain held for a grouped editorial decision. All imported entities remain `DRAFT` until the authenticated publication workflow can record an accountable release action.
