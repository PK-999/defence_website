# Gallantry awardee enrichment source ledger

Research completed 13 September 2026 for the six Chakra-series categories collected by `src/scratch.py`. The scraper uses the Ministry of Defence Gallantry Awards portal as the canonical row source and follows the official profile, photo, and citation assets linked from each awardee page.

The recommended field name is `Gallantry Action Date`: it identifies the date of the act for which the decoration was awarded and avoids the ambiguity of `Action Date`. `Service Entry Date` is used instead of `Service Joining Date` because official records may describe the event as enrolment or commission.

## Extraction and conflict policy

- Direct values are copied only when present in an identified source. Blank, `N/A`, unavailable, or unextractable values become `Not documented` in enrichment fields.
- `Gallantry Action Date` is parsed from the official `Award / Date of Action` value or profile PDF. `Awarded Date` is populated only when a source explicitly states a conferral, presentation, or notification date; the award year and action date are not substituted.
- Biography and `Citation Details` come from the official profile PDF when its text is extractable. A citation image remains linked in `Citation`; it is not silently represented as transcribed prose.
- `Source URLs` records the official awardee page and linked media/evidence URLs used for that row. The separate source list above records the research source families and their known coverage.
- Photos remain linked to the publisher's official asset URL. No image is copied into `public/`, and reuse rights are not assumed merely because the image is government-hosted.
