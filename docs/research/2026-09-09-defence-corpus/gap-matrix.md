The portal index returned 4,310 observations but only 2,778 distinct directory IDs, including only 16 Param Vir Chakra records. Successful pagination is therefore not proof of coverage. Its date field also has unresolved semantics. The portal policy restricts systematic copying without permission; bulk profile retrieval was not started. The previously collected directory is retained as incomplete research evidence, not a publication feed.

The 2018 transcription has 4,191 rows (PVC 21; MVC 218; VrC 1,319; AC 87; KC 478; SC 2,068). These are historical source rows, not certified current counts or unique people. A biography link, an award listing and an official citation are separate evidence types.

## Publication conditions

Every public fact needs a source and locator, appropriate date and entity scope. Leave unknown values null with a reason. Do not populate missing biographies with generic prose. Do not publish discovery-list technical specifications as verified facts. Use public historical deployments; avoid inferring operational availability from ownership. Preserve civilian recipients and historical decoration names. Honour recipients' real names, ranks and award titles even within the site's spy theme.
